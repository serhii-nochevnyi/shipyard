# Decisions

## Replace every proposed Codex Luna/max placement with GPT-6.1 Sol/low
**Why:** The operator explicitly selected the stronger baseline with lower reasoning effort to reduce repairs and cross-model transitions. The proposal applies this to executor, initial ci-fix/review-fix, drift-check and routine outer coordination.
**What was rejected:** Retaining Luna/max as the new default; inferring quota savings solely from lower effort.
**Scope fence:** Preserve historical controls. Luna/medium sentinel is unchanged. Native judgment and escalation use Sol 6.1/high or xhigh. Outer coordination is a separate existing owner seam, not automatically part of native-role evidence.

## Replace every proposed Claude Opus 5.5/medium placement with Sonnet 5.5/xhigh
**Why:** The operator explicitly selected Sonnet 5.5's capability for research, decomposition, independent review, integration, critical/checkpoint execution, repair repeat and substantive outer coordination.
**What was rejected:** Retaining Opus/medium in those proposed placements; equating benchmark strength with proven outcome quality or quota economy.
**Scope fence:** Pin claude-sonnet-5-5. Opus 5.5/high remains capability escalation. Preserve independent review, complete instructions, critical/checkpoint gates and authenticated repair predecessor identity.

## Deliver the ladder separately from long-term economy experiments
**Why:** The operator explicitly asks to investigate, decompose and execute promptly in parallel where safe. Model compatibility and quality-gate correctness can be tested now; causal quota savings need later observations.
**What was rejected:** Ad hoc edits in stale dirty main; waiting seven days before even building a reviewable policy migration; silently weakening existing gates or changing shared active hosts.
**Scope fence:** An accepted policy amendment authorizes the selected versioned ladder; no savings percentage, measurement exemption, arbitrary runtime override or experiment auto-promotion is implied. Use an isolated graph and existing host, preserve ongoing phase-43/45 work, and stop on genuine host refusal.
