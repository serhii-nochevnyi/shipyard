## Coherent phase-41 scope candidate for the human to decide

The smallest coherent candidate is: **A/C: integration and installed-path verification of phase-39 work, with only reproduced residual fixes; B: bounded caller adoption of existing handoff at phase/long-wait boundaries; D: identity-bound candidate and trusted finalization resume; E: local projection dependency fingerprints; F: existing usage/overhead report joined to verified outcomes.** This follows `.planning/phases/41-reduce-pipeline-subscription-overhead/CONTEXT.md:47-152` and the measured priority order in `docs/audits/2026-09-25-claude-session-efficiency.md:119-128`; checked with `nl -ba` on those ranges. For A, the retained-constraint proof matters as much as reduced bytes: a superseded ADR may replace only selected decisions, and a transitive reference may still govern. For B, a small checkpoint must carry ownership, attempts and pending gates rather than just a summary. For C, a shell wait/poll and a model response are separate counters. For D, base movement invalidates affected verification and cannot be represented as a successful host result. For E, an aggregate may legitimately change when one plan changes. For F, a functional fix can ship with an **inconclusive** efficiency verdict until the existing cohort gate is met. These are acceptance conditions in `CONTEXT.md:47-152`, not observed outcomes.
