## Goal

A ticket can carry `delivery.preauthorized: true` — the record of a human having
approved this specific ticket's risk at decomposition time. Gate 2 validates it
and carries it into `tickets.json`. This ticket adds the RECORD only; nothing
reads it yet (T-21-02 does).

## Context (Reads)

- ADR-001 **D6**: "Every high-risk approval arriving at 3am is a decision a
  person could have made while approving the ticket set. Risk classes are
  pre-authorized at decomposition; anything not pre-authorized defers to the
  morning front rather than blocking."
- **The field case this phase exists for, observed 2026-08-25 in this repo.**
  T-22-04 (`risk: high`) reached green, conform, undrafted — and stopped. T-22-05
  was stacked on it and stopped too, correctly (a child never lands into an open
  checkpoint parent). Both sat until a human typed one word. Nothing in either
  diff was a surprise at that moment: the risk had been assessed when the plan
  was written. That word belonged at decomposition.
- `validate-graph.cjs:374` — `if (t.risk === 'high' && !t.human_checkpoint)` is
  an ERROR. **Keep it.** The two flags mean different things and this ticket must
  not collapse them: `human_checkpoint` = "a human must act on this";
  `preauthorized` = "the human already did, at plan time". A high-risk ticket
  still declares that it needs human judgment; pre-authorization records that the
  judgment was supplied early.
- `validate-graph.cjs:158` — where `human_checkpoint: delivery.human_checkpoint === true`
  is normalized out of the frontmatter; `:482` and `:523` — where it enters
  `tickets.json` and `tickets.yaml`. The new field follows the same three places.
- `frontmatter.cjs` — the YAML-subset parser. It "either represents a construct
  or reports a structured error; it never half-parses". A misspelled or
  non-boolean value must therefore be an ERROR here, not a silent false: this
  field's whole purpose is to authorize an unattended merge, so a typo that reads
  as "not authorized" is safe, but one that reads as "authorized" would be the
  worst possible failure. Fail closed and say so.

## Scope

- `delivery.preauthorized` is parsed, defaults to `false` when absent, and is
  written into `tickets.json` and `tickets.yaml` beside `human_checkpoint`.
- Only a literal `true`/`false` is accepted. Anything else — a string, a number,
  `yes`, an empty value — is a Gate 2 ERROR naming the ticket and the field.
- `preauthorized: true` on a ticket WITHOUT `human_checkpoint: true` is an
  error too: authorizing a stop that does not exist is a planning mistake, and
  silently ignoring it would let a plan claim an approval that means nothing.
- No behaviour changes anywhere else. No script reads the field in this ticket.

## Out of scope

`sentinel.cjs`, `front.cjs` (T-21-02), `decompose.md` (T-21-03), and the epic →
integration-branch merge, which pre-authorization must NEVER cover — that
boundary belongs to T-21-02's tests.

## Acceptance criteria

- A plan with `delivery.preauthorized: true` and `human_checkpoint: true`
  validates, and `tickets.json` carries `preauthorized: true`.
- Absent field → `preauthorized: false` in `tickets.json`, no warning.
- A non-boolean value fails Gate 2, naming ticket and field.
- `preauthorized: true` without `human_checkpoint: true` fails Gate 2.
- Every existing graph fixture still validates unchanged.

## Test strategy

Cases in `graph-validator-smoke.sh` beside the existing `human_checkpoint` ones.
Each new rejection needs a matching ACCEPT case, so the tests prove the rule
discriminates rather than just refuses.
