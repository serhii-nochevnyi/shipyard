## Goal

The guard runs the detector during arch-review and records its result in the
`gate_status:` trailer beside the architecture verdict. Nothing merges
differently because of it — and that non-blocking property is pinned by a test,
not by a sentence.

## Context (Reads)

- `sentinel.cjs:151` — the trailer parser takes the LAST line matching
  `^gate_status:` and splits it on commas into key=value parts. So an extra
  `degenerate-green=<n|clean>` part is compatible **by construction**. That is
  precisely why it must be TESTED: a compatibility that holds by construction is
  the kind nobody notices breaking. `sentinel.cjs:497` is the merge gate's
  requirement — it demands `arch-review=conform` and must keep passing with the
  longer trailer.
- `sentinel.cjs:318` and `:330` — the duty texts that name the trailer. They will
  read a little differently once it carries two keys; keep them accurate.
- **The non-blocking rule needs a mechanical guard.** CLAUDE.md's standing
  lesson, earned repeatedly in this programme: prose rules get skipped,
  mechanical gates hold. A comment saying "reporting only" is exactly the kind of
  claim this repository has shipped and then contradicted in code — the `BEHIND`
  comment, the `plan_defect` lifting message, `deliver.md`'s stale REQUIRED list.
  The test is the artefact; the sentence is decoration.
- **Journalling is what makes "earns blocking status from field data" real.**
  Without an accumulating record, that phrase is unfalsifiable. Findings go to
  the journal per ticket so `pipeline-stats` can eventually answer "how often did
  it fire, and how often was it right".
- **The Workflow path bypasses this file's prose.** Anything an agent must do
  belongs where that agent reads — `pr-sentinel.md` for the guard. A rule that
  lives only in `deliver.md` is not in force on the Workflow path.
- CLAUDE.md: name MECHANISMS, never contrast runtimes by name — the generator
  rewrites that word and inverts the meaning.
- Both manifests move together to 0.41.0, or `make test-overlay` fails on drift.

## Scope

- `pr-sentinel.md`: the arch-review step runs `degenerate-green.cjs` and appends
  `degenerate-green=clean|<n>` to the trailer it already writes. Findings are
  reported in the PR body as a short list a human can skim, never as a reason to
  withhold `conform`.
- `deliver.md`: the trailer's documented shape gains the key; the model-policy
  and gate sections stay untouched.
- Findings are journalled per ticket.
- `tests/unit/trailer.test.cjs` (new): the merge gate still accepts a trailer
  carrying both keys; a trailer reporting findings does NOT change any merge
  verdict; a malformed extra part does not break the `arch-review` read.
- Version bump in both manifests.

## Out of scope

The detector itself (T-21-04), `sentinel.cjs` and `front.cjs` (T-21-02 owns the
guard's code this phase), and — emphatically — making anything block.

## Acceptance criteria

- A PR body with `gate_status: arch-review=conform, degenerate-green=3, checks=green`
  merges exactly as one with only `arch-review=conform` does.
- The same body with `degenerate-green=clean` behaves identically.
- A trailer whose extra part is malformed still yields `arch-review=conform`.
- Findings appear in the journal, readable beside the ticket's other events.
- `make test-fast` and `make test-docs` pass; both manifest versions equal.

## Test strategy

Drive `sentinel.cjs`'s own trailer reader and merge gate with each body form.
The decisive assertion is EQUIVALENCE — the verdict with findings must equal the
verdict without them — rather than "does not throw".

- `node tests/unit/trailer.test.cjs`
- `make test-sentinel`
