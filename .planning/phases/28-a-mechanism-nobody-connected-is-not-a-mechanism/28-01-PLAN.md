## Goal

Two guards posted over different waves are two agents. T-27-01 collapsed a
guard's N records to one agent correctly, and keyed the collapse on the ROLE
STRING, which made two guards indistinguishable — turning the cap's error
direction from under-dispatch to over-dispatch.

## Context (Reads)

- ADR-007 D1 and its Family A.
- `front.cjs`'s `agentsInFlight`: `if (how === 'round') perRound.add(role)` — a
  `Set` on the role. `AGENT_CARDINALITY` maps `pr-sentinel` to `'round'`, and
  `DEFAULT_CARDINALITY` is `'ticket'` so an unknown role spends a whole agent,
  which is the correct direction for a dispatch gate and must not change.
- `front.cjs:321`'s own comment: the records `activeDispatches` returns are
  `{ticket: {role, at}}` — no identity, no wave, no group.
- `deliver.md`'s guard step sanctions the state: "Re-post a guard for PRs opened
  after it started (or hand them to the running one with `SendMessage`); do not
  leave a PR unguarded."
- `.planning/backlog/phase-26-followups.md`, the paragraph justifying the OLD
  record-counting error by its direction — "the failure direction is safe".
  Under-count is the other direction, and that is why this is not a tidy-up.

## Scope

- **A dispatch record carries the identity of the agent that holds it.**
  `dispatch-record.cjs mark` takes it and stores it; `activeDispatches` returns
  it. A record written without one is not rejected — an older board must still
  read — but it counts as its own agent, because unknown resolves UPWARD here for
  the same reason `DEFAULT_CARDINALITY` does.
- **`agentsInFlight` counts unique live agents.** A `'round'` role contributes
  one per distinct identity rather than one per role. One guard over four PRs is
  still one; two guards over two PRs each are two.
- `deliver.md` passes the identity on every `mark`, at both the executor
  hand-out and the guard hand-over.

## Out of scope

Per-role or per-model weighting of the cap — still no cost model. Changing
`AGENT_CARDINALITY`'s vocabulary or the `'ticket'` default. Any change to the
three capacity readers' agreement, which T-27-01 established and this ticket
must not disturb.

## Acceptance criteria

- One guard holding four tickets: `in_flight: 1`. Two guards, one holding two
  tickets and one holding one, plus two executors: `in_flight: 4`. On base the
  second board reports 3, so the test fails before the fix — that is the defect.
- A record with no identity counts as its own agent, and a board mixing
  identified and unidentified records never counts an unidentified one as free.
- **Time is not an identity**: a board where new tickets were added to an
  already-running guard (same identity, later `at`) still reports one agent.
  Assert this explicitly — `at` is the tempting substitute and it is wrong.
- `front.cjs`, `ci-wait.cjs` and `stop-gate.cjs` still agree on a full board
  (T-27-01's invariant), asserted from one shared fixture.
- **Mutation check.** Restore the role-keyed `Set` and the two-guard case fails.
