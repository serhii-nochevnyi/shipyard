## Scope

- A resolved entry is NOT deleted. It gains a `**RESOLVED**` line naming the
  release and the ticket that closed it, and keeps its body — the reasoning is
  why the fix took the shape it did, and deleting it loses the record of a
  decision. History is the point of a backlog, not its contents.
- An entry that is wholly resolved (`no-ci-…`) says so at the TOP, in its first
  line, so a skimming reader cannot miss it.
- Entries still live say so explicitly. Silence must not be how a reader infers
  status.
- Add a one-screen `README.md`? NO — out of scope; do not invent structure.
