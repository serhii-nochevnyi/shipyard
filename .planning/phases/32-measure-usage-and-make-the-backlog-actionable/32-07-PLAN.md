## Goal

Make transcript usage comparable at ticket and dispatch level while keeping
provider, model and effort evidence explicit. This closes the measurement seam
identified by the 2026-09-10 limit audit; it does not claim quota savings.

## Context (Reads)

ADR-011 D1, D8 and D11; ADR-011-ROLLOUT OPT-03; the T-32-03 through T-32-05
dispatch contracts; the T-32-01 usage collector; and the audit finding that
raw transcripts cannot prove which launch consumed them.

## Scope

Give every dispatch a unique `dispatch_id`, with an explicit override for
replays, and persist a separate append-only usage-attribution ledger keyed by a
session, request or message identity. Validate provider/runtime pairs, roles,
task levels, effort states and metadata before one locked append; make retries
idempotent and later observations revisioned. Extend the read-only usage report
to join Claude and Codex transcripts to the ledger, preserve cumulative and
partial counter semantics, expose attribution and efficiency coverage, and
exclude ambiguous or incomplete rows. Document the handoff in the delivery and
sentinel contracts and include both bundle smoke surfaces.

## Out of scope

Automatic transcript discovery or collection, subscription allowance or API
pricing conversion, model/effort policy changes, live-session restart logic,
Jira writes, prompt/tool payload storage, and treating a stop marker as a
verified delivery outcome.

## Acceptance criteria

- Single and batched dispatch records carry distinct safe `dispatch_id` values;
  explicit ids are preserved and cannot be active on two tickets.
- Attribution records reject unknown fields, provider/runtime mismatches,
  invalid roles/efforts and missing transcript identity before touching the
  ledger; identical retries append nothing, while changed facts append a higher
  revision under one lock.
- Claude and Codex transcript joins use exact message/request identity when
  available and session identity only when unambiguous. Ambiguous, mismatched,
  malformed and unattributed rows remain visible and are excluded from
  efficiency-ready counts.
- Cumulative Codex snapshots and late Claude updates are not double-counted;
  unknown or unsupported counters stay unknown, and advisor usage remains a
  separate kind.
- Reports expose requested, applied and observed model/effort plus dispatch,
  ticket and completion coverage without converting the result into quota or
  savings claims. Existing read-only and batch dispatch behavior remains intact.
- The unit tests, docs smoke and installed bundle smoke pass.

## Test strategy

Exercise atomic/idempotent ledger writes, provider and schema validation,
exact/session/ambiguous joins, model mismatch, cumulative snapshots and
redaction. Extend dispatch subprocess coverage for generated and explicit ids;
run the usage, dispatch, contract and bundle smoke suites.
