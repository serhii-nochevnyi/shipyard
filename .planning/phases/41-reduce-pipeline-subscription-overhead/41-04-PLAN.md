## Goal

REQ-154 accepts the observed race in which every parent `wait_agent` call times out and the typed native child completes asynchronously afterwards. It keeps native child completion verification mandatory.

Split on 2026-09-25 (operator decision): the authenticated finalization candidate, the OS-sandboxed trusted verification producer, the HMAC records and the recovery-only `--resume-finalization` CLI moved to T-42-01 (phase 42, REQ-158). Two executor runs could not exercise real OS sandbox denial inside the executor's own Seatbelt sandbox, and that work also carries the blocking human checkpoint.

## Scope

- Accept a completed typed native child after all parent `wait_agent` calls returned timeout. In `codex-runtime-host.cjs`, parent spawn parsing validates every wait/output pair and boolean timeout status, without requiring the literal successful wait message. The launcher still must pass `readNativeCodexChild` / `parseNativeChildTranscript`: exactly one completion, exact parent/task/role/model/effort/instructions and stable transcript. A successful wait alone is never proof of execution. Preserve blocked handbacks and artifact containment checks. The local phase-41 bootstrap proves this observed race; it is not a shipped implementation.

## Out of scope

Everything in T-42-01: candidate persistence, trusted verification producer, HMAC records, finalizer `expectedTree` and recovery-only CLI. Also automatic executor replay.
