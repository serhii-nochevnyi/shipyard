## Goal

REQ-152 gives an operator an explicit safe handoff at a completed phase boundary or durable long wait, with enough bounded evidence to continue without replaying completed work.

## Context (Reads)

- P41-B in `CONTEXT.md`; `41-RESEARCH.md:247-250`.
- `plugins/delivery-pipeline/scripts/session-handoff.cjs:510-615,799-880` already validates metadata, pins worktree artifact digests, fences pending launches and acknowledges successor after live revalidation. Extend this controller, not a second store.
- `plugins/delivery-pipeline/scripts/orchestration-overhead.cjs:23-34` already names checkpoint collection, startup and cache warmup stages; its implementation remains with T-41-06.
- `tests/unit/session-handoff.test.cjs` is the existing hermetic controller fixture.

## Scope

- Define a 32 KiB UTF-8 serialized checkpoint bound for `current_snapshot` plus decision/finding/attempt/gate metadata, and a 64 KiB bound for the whole checkpoint. Larger evidence is referenced by path and SHA-256 through existing `artifact_refs` inside the owned worktree; missing or changed pins refuse with a named recovery action. Keep secrets, raw prompts and transcripts forbidden.
- Require accepted decisions, unresolved findings, attempt history, pending gate IDs, owner/run/ticket identity, artifact digests and exact `next_action` in the bounded snapshot or pinned references. Preserve predecessor history and observation IDs through resume.
- Permit checkpoint only at an explicit completed phase boundary or an owned durable long wait after pending launch reservations are reconciled and child enumeration proves no active child. Successor must revalidate live HEAD/base/worktree, reviews/checks and child enumeration before compare-and-swap acknowledgement. No threshold-driven transfer.

## Out of scope

Automatic session rotation, new scheduler and conversation-copy handoff.
