## Goal

Research workers are told the 500-character summary bound at launch, and an
over-long summary is bounded deterministically before the trusted consumer
seals it, with a notice naming the line and the original length, so verified
research is kept (D-12; bound chosen over refuse per `39-CONTEXT.md`).
