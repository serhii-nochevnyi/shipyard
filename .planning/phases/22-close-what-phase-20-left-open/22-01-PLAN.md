## Goal

A `plan_defect` park currently tells the human the OPPOSITE of its own rule. The
lifting rule must come from the store that owns the park, so that adding a kind
cannot leave a renderer describing it with another kind's semantics.

## Context (Reads)

- The contradiction, all three lines shipped in the same epic:

      escalation-record.cjs:318   "… Moving the PR does NOT lift it."
      front.cjs:121               "It lifts by itself once the PR moves (a push,
                                   a review answer, undrafting)"
      sentinel.cjs:186            "(lifts when the PR moves; `… clear <id>`)"

- `front.cjs` and `sentinel.cjs` contain **zero** occurrences of `plan_defect`
  (measured on the merged epic). T-20-03's new kind falls through to the generic
  escalation string, so the board states a falsehood to the one person who has
  to act on it at 3am.
- `escalation-record.cjs` — `activeEscalations(cwd, state)` is the shared reader
  both renderers call, and it already carries the record. The lifting rule
  differs by kind: an ordinary escalation lifts when the PR MOVES (fingerprint
  of status/draft/review_decision/checks); a `plan_defect` lifts only when the
  PLAN changes (plan_hash) or on an explicit `clear`.
- `sentinel.cjs:187` — the `DRIFTED` line is the pattern to copy: it already
  states a kind-specific rule ("re-plan it; the park lifts when the plan
  changes"). The bug is that `plan_defect` has no such line, not that the design
  lacks a place for one.
- CLAUDE.md, "Whatever the front parks, `sentinel.cjs` must park too" — the two
  run concurrently off the same state and must agree. A message fixed in one and
  not the other is the same defect in a new place.
- Reuse check (§5): do NOT add a second `why` map or a kind→text table at each
  render site. The duplication at two sites IS what let them drift; the text
  must be produced once, beside the data.

## Scope

- `escalation-record.cjs` exports the human-readable lifting sentence for a
  record, derived from its kind — one function, one home.
- `front.cjs` and `sentinel.cjs` consume it instead of composing their own
  string. Their remaining job is placement, not wording.
- An unknown/absent kind falls back to the ordinary-escalation wording, because
  every record written before this change has no kind field and must keep
  rendering sensibly.
- The `clear` hint stays in both messages; it is the one remedy that works for
  every kind.

## Out of scope

Changing WHEN a park lifts (the expiry logic is correct and tested), the
`parentIsMoving` checkpoint exception, and any bucket routing.

## Acceptance criteria

- A `plan_defect` park renders, in both `front.cjs` and `sentinel.cjs`, a
  sentence naming re-planning — and containing no claim that a PR move lifts it.
- An ordinary escalation renders unchanged from today, word for word.
- A record with no `kind` renders as an ordinary escalation.
- A test asserts the two renderers produce the SAME sentence for the same
  record — the drift itself, not just the current wording.

## Test strategy

Assert on CONTENT, not on a message equal to a literal: pin that the
`plan_defect` text does not contain "PR moves" and does mention the plan. A
literal-equality test would pass on a rewrite that reintroduces the falsehood.
