## Goal

D-04 (REQ-162). Under `auto_merge: epic` a checkpoint blocked merges even into the epic; three tickets waited about 2.5 h. `human_checkpoint` takes `review` (a human approves the PR, then the guard merges into the epic) or `merge` (the human merges; also the value for external-dependency holds). `true` keeps meaning `merge`, `preauthorized: true` keeps working, Gate 2 states the consequence of each value, and `pipeline-stats` keeps attributing guard merges separately. Children of a `review` parent cascade under today's rule (P-04).

Risk is high because it changes who may merge; the checkpoint verifies a `review` ticket on an operator-owned scratch repository.

## Scope

- `validate-graph.cjs`: accept `delivery.human_checkpoint` as `true`, `false`, `review` or `merge`; any other value is an error naming the allowed values (never silently false). `human_checkpoint` stays boolean (true for `review`, `merge`, `true`); new field `checkpoint`: `'merge'` for `true`/`merge`, `'review'` for `review`, `null` otherwise; written to `tickets.json` and `tickets.yaml`. High risk accepts either value; `preauthorized` still requires a checkpoint.
- `front.cjs` `needsHuman`: `merge` → true unless preauthorized (today); `review` → true until the board row shows a human APPROVED review on the head.
- `sentinel.cjs` `mergeOne`: `checkpoint === 'merge'` keeps today's refusal (and `preauthorized` path). `checkpoint === 'review'` requires an APPROVED review whose `commit_id` is the head, re-verified live, from a positively human reviewer: the review `user.type` is `User`, the login does not end in `[bot]`, matches no built-in or configured bot identity (T-43-08), and is not the PR author. An unknown or missing `user.type` does not authorize the merge (fail closed; T-43-08 "unknown author counts as human" applies only where it makes freshness stricter, never here); then the guard merges into the epic and journals `merge` with `checkpoint: 'review'`. Without it, hold with the reason `awaiting human review`. The open-checkpoint-parent hold is unchanged for both values (P-04).
- `pipeline-stats.cjs`: attribute `checkpoint_review_merge` separately from `checkpoint_preauthorized_merge` and `checkpoint_unauthorized_merge`.
- `decompose.md` Gate 2 and both delivery-rules copies: state the consequence of each value (`review`: a human approves, the guard merges into the epic; `merge`: the human merges, used for external-dependency holds; `true` = `merge`).
- Tests:
