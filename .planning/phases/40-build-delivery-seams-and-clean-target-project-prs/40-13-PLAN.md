## Goal

When one of the four investigation research lines fails (D-11):

- the other lines' sealed artifacts are kept;
- the result names the failed line and its real cause (not `planning research has invalid scope or result` plus a generic repair text);
- the operator can re-dispatch only that line;
- the fan-out as a whole stays failed until all four lines are sealed.

## Scope

- `investigation-research.mjs`:
  - Catch per line. A failed line returns `{line, status: 'failed', code, cause}`, where `cause` is the boundary's own error message and code, unwrapped from the adapter's repair suffix.
  - A sealed line returns its artifact reference.
  - The fan-out result is `blocked` via `researchLineFailure` when any line failed, and it is never a success.
  - Accept an optional `lines: [<one line id>]` argument: run only that line, and require the manifests of the other three lines to already exist and pass `sealResearch`'s per-line checks.
- `claude-delivery-host.cjs` `cliDispatch`: accept either four lines or exactly one line plus proof of the other three sealed artifacts (verified through the sealer, not trusted from the request). The final envelope is produced only when all four pass.
- `planning-result-sealer.cjs`: add `verifySealedLine({root, scope, line})` used by the single-line path. Make `researchLineFailure` carry the adapter-free cause.
- Tests:
  - `tests/unit/investigation-research.test.cjs`: one failing line leaves three sealed, the result is blocked and names the line and cause; a single-line re-dispatch after that produces the full envelope; a single-line request with a missing sibling refuses naming the sibling.
  - `tests/unit/claude-delivery-host.test.cjs`: the host accepts the single-line form only with the three verified siblings.
  - `tests/unit/planning-result-sealer.test.cjs`: `verifySealedLine` accepts a sealed line and refuses a tampered one.
- Comment policy on added lines.

## Out of scope

- `investigate.md` prose for re-dispatching one line (T-40-25).

## Acceptance criteria

- No research failure surfaces only `planning research has invalid scope or result`. Every failure names the line and the underlying code.
- The fan-out never reports success with fewer than four sealed lines.
- Re-dispatching one line does not re-run or rewrite the three sealed lines (asserted by artifact mtimes or digests).
