## Goal

`/shipyard:deliver` tells the orchestrator to use the seams instead of doing their work by hand (D-01, D-03, D-04, D-05, D-07, D-08, D-20):

- dispatch through `deliver-dispatch.cjs launch`, then `wait`/`status`, with no hand-assembled request, no ticket `type` in signals, and no foreground polling;
- rely on the host-issued in-flight record, and mark the durable dispatch only after the verified receipt;
- run the sentinel through its preflight;
- publish target-project PRs through `pr-hygiene.cjs check`, then `gh pr create`, then `pr-ledger.cjs record`, with a conventional title and a neutral body;
- read gate verdicts from the `merge-gate` status.

The Shipyard repository keeps its `Ticket:` publication convention.
