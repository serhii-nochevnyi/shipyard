## Goal

Say WHERE the projection runs, prove the loop actually calls it, and state the
one asymmetry this ADR claims: a pending projection is neither actionable nor a
reason to block.

## Context (Reads)

- ADR-008 D6 and D7.
- `tests/unit/judgment-contract.test.cjs:224` — the `WIRED` table: seven rows
  today, six `kind: 'wired'` and one `decided`. Read the header at line 50 and
  the warning at line 83 ("a guard that certifies whatever it is pointed at is
  worse than no guard") before adding a row. Each existing row names a
  `mechanism`, its `reader`, its `homes` with a caller regex, and a `why` that
  states what breaks when the call is absent.
- CLAUDE.md on `state-sync.cjs` — its wall time IS the conveyor's tick rate.
  That is the reason the projection is a separate act and not a step inside it.
- `README.md:288` and `deliver.md:467` — the config key list, in prose, in two
  places, agreeing with `capability.json` by hand and by nothing else. ADR-008
  records that as a real instance of "a guard asserts a sweep, not a list" and
  puts the sweep itself out of scope; this ticket does the third manual edit and
  says so plainly rather than pretending the list is maintained.

## Scope

- `deliver.md`: the projection is a separate act AFTER a state-sync, not inside
  it. State the asymmetry explicitly and with its justification, because it
  contradicts how every other unwired mechanism in this repo was fixed: a
  pending projection is **not actionable and not a reason to block**, and the
  reason is specific rather than general — the watermark makes catch-up free, so
  a skipped round costs one round of tracker lag and the next round closes it.
  There is no equivalent here of the 5h46m silence, because nothing waits on it.
- A new `WIRED` row: mechanism `jira-project.cjs plan`, reader = the tracker
  projection, home = `deliver.md` with a caller regex matching the call, `why` =
  without it the planner is a script with no caller, which is ADR-007's subject
  and the exact defect phase 28 shipped a fix for.
- `README.md:288` and `deliver.md:467`: add `jira_transitions` to both key
  lists.

## Out of scope

- A sweep asserting the three key-list homes agree. Named in ADR-008 as a
  follow-up; it is about config documentation in general, not this feature.
- Any change to `state-sync.cjs`, `front.cjs` or `stop-gate.cjs`. The projection
  being invisible to them is achieved by NOT touching them, and this ticket
  declares none of those files precisely so that a diff proves it.
