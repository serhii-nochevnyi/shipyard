## Goal

Create the single versioned, runtime-neutral policy and the immutable
resolve/validate/launch/receipt tracer that every later adapter will consume.
Keep concrete Codex and Claude mappings in a runtime-adapter module, outside the
fingerprinted logical policy.

## Context (Reads)

- `.planning/architecture/ADR-014-mandatory-runtime-model-ladder.md`
- `.planning/architecture/ADR-014-INTERFACES.md`
- `.planning/architecture/ADR-014-DATA-MODEL.md`
- `plugins/delivery-pipeline/scripts/pipeline-config.cjs`
- `plugins/delivery-pipeline/scripts/dispatch-record.cjs`

## Scope

Implement all nine roles and the requested rungs, runtime-adapter mappings,
signal evaluation, policy fingerprinting, and a boundary with injected adapters
for tests. Resolution must retain every fired signal, distinguish requested
from applied values, and require the immediately preceding boundary-verified
receipt before repair escalation; a committed successor must make its
predecessor non-replayable across restart recovery. Static Codex dispatch must
validate generated agent existence, content, policy binding, and digest before
launch.

## Acceptance criteria

- `research`, `decomposition`, `executor`, `pr-sentinel`, `integrator`,
  `drift-check`, `arch-review`, `ci-fix`, and `review-fix` resolve exactly as
  ADR-014 specifies.
- Fixed Luna roles do not promote from global risk, checkpoint, or window
  signals; judgement and repair roles do.
- Unknown runtime, conflicting override, unsupported selection, or missing
  receipt fails closed.
- Resolution contains policy version/hash, runtime, role, logical rung,
  concrete model/effort, route, backend, mechanism, and signal reasons.
