## Goal

Report policy resolution, runtime application, provider observation, and usage
join coverage as separate facts.

## Context (Reads)

- `plugins/delivery-pipeline/scripts/dispatch-record.cjs`
- `plugins/delivery-pipeline/scripts/pipeline-stats.cjs`
- `plugins/delivery-pipeline/scripts/usage-attribution.cjs`
- `.planning/architecture/ADR-014-DATA-MODEL.md`

## Scope

Reconcile receipts against policy fingerprints and launch mechanisms. Surface
stale policy, contradictory application, missing receipt, unknown observation,
and legacy records independently. Aggregate by runtime, role, rung, concrete
model, effort, and fired signal without relabeling history.

## Acceptance criteria

- Reports distinguish resolved, applied, observed, and usage-joined states.
- A missing or unverifiable receipt is visible and never counted as compliant.
- Both Codex and Claude dimensions are represented without changing Claude’s
  palette.
